Privacy Policy

Your notes live on your Mac. Turn on iCloud to sync them, and choose which notes to share.

What stays on your Mac

Your notes, attachments, folders, Trash, and settings are stored on your Mac. You do not need a Fei Notes account to use a local library.

Fei Notes reads the files you select for import. You choose where to save exports and backups. Backups may also include local copies of shared notes and changes waiting to sync.

iCloud sync and sharing

When you turn on iCloud, Fei Notes uses Apple’s CloudKit to store notes, attachments, folders, and change records in your private iCloud database. An iCloud account identifier keeps each account’s library separate. Device names, online status, and collaborators’ cursor and selection information also sync through CloudKit.

When you invite someone, the Apple Account email you enter is sent to CloudKit to find the recipient. They can access the shared copy and its attachments with the permissions you grant. Other notes and settings stay in their respective libraries.

The TestFlight beta includes cloud features without a trial or subscription. In the App Store version, starting a trial saves its start date in your private iCloud database. Apple handles subscription payments. Fei Notes uses StoreKit to verify purchases and expiration dates so it can enable cloud features or restore purchases.

Web images and external links

Web images in your notes are loaded from the websites that host them. With automatic downloads enabled, those sites receive image requests that may include your IP address and other connection information. You can turn off Download web images in Settings → General and load images individually.

When you open an external link, the destination website handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Diagnostics and feedback

Fei Notes keeps startup, sync, and error logs on your Mac. These may include file paths. The app itself does not automatically send these local logs to Fei Notes support.

During TestFlight testing, Apple collects crash reports and usage information and shares them with the developer, along with any feedback you submit. We use this information to investigate problems and improve Fei Notes. See TestFlight & Privacy.

If you contact support by email or Telegram, the messages, screenshots, and logs you send are handled by those services and used to respond to your request.

Deletion and backups

Deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days, unless you delete them permanently sooner. Sync records, shared copies, and exported backups may have their own retention periods. Emptying Trash does not also remove those copies.

Switching to a local library, signing out of iCloud, or canceling a subscription keeps your existing iCloud data. A shared note’s owner can revoke access. Copies that someone has already saved separately remain with that person. See how to delete notes or stop syncing.
