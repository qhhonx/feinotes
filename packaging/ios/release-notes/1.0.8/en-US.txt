Fei Notes 1.0.8 beta for iPhone and iPad

• Create and organize documents on iPhone. Keep the document list beside the editor on iPad.
• Share original documents or folders and edit together in real time. Document and sync formats match the Mac beta.

Try editing with the keyboard, searching for a document, and writing offline. On iPad, switch between portrait and landscape and check the document list and editor. If you also test on Mac, update every test device to the 1.0.8 beta before checking that changes to the same sample document sync.
