Fei Notes 1.0.8 (1018) for Mac

• Fixed cases where an invalid cloud record could block synchronization of other documents.
• Fixed a crash when replaying cloud changes after reopening the app, while retaining pending edits and conflicts.
• Improved shared-document navigation, organization and member controls.

Update all participating devices to this round of builds. Please test editing the same sample document across devices, working offline, reopening the app and collaborating through an invitation to a specific Apple Account.

Known limitations: some existing library catalogues and individual document conflicts still need separate recovery. Recovery work in progress is not included here. Creating or retrying public sharing links remains unavailable; private invitations to specific accounts remain available. Please preserve your library and report any problems.

iCloud access is included during TestFlight testing without a subscription.
