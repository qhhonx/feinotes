# Security and data-loss reports

Send private vulnerability reports and reports involving personal notes to [support@feinotes.app](mailto:support@feinotes.app).

Include the app version and build number, macOS version, a description of the affected workflow, and synthetic reproduction steps where possible. Remove Apple Account identifiers, personal note content and access tokens from logs before sharing them. Keep private keys and signing material private.

Issues that can lose notes, mix Apple Accounts or bypass sharing permissions receive priority. Acknowledge the report, preserve reproduction evidence, and coordinate a fix and disclosure with the reporter.
