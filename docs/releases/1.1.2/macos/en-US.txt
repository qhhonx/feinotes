Fei Notes 1.1.2 beta

This release integrates upstream editor improvements and consistent controls for sidebar menus, invitations, and sharing access. Shared and unshared states are clearer, document menus preserve the open document, and shared-folder sorting and drag operations have been refined.

Manage participants and link access separately, and create public invitations for shared folders. New invitations use the updated Fei Notes link format; previously sent invitations still open.

Please test editing in the Lark and Notes styles; organizing personal and shared folders and opening sidebar menus; continuing on another device and editing together; creating, opening, and closing invitations; and opening the personal original after stopping sharing.

iCloud sync and real-time collaboration are included during TestFlight testing without a subscription.
