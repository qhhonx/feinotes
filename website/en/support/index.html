Start writing

Open Fei Notes and create a document. Local documents work offline and do not require an account.

Import your Quiet.md documents

In Settings → Library, choose Import from Quiet.md and select your existing library folder. Fei Notes copies the documents and attachments into your current library and keeps the original folder.

Continue on another device

Sign in to the same Apple Account on your devices. In Fei Notes, go to Settings → Library (Documents in older builds) and follow the prompts to turn on iCloud sync. When you first enable sync, you can copy your local documents to iCloud.

On your other device, turn on iCloud sync and let your documents download. If Fei Notes asks you to restart, quit and reopen the app.

iCloud sync and collaboration are free during TestFlight testing.

A document has not synced yet

Check the sync status in Settings → Library. Make sure you are connected to the internet, signed in to iCloud, and have enough storage, then choose Sync Now. Changes waiting to upload stay on this device.

If you see Items That Couldn’t Sync, follow the message on the original device. Check that the attachment is still there and can be read. Replace files larger than 64 MiB with smaller ones. If you changed iCloud accounts, restart Fei Notes when prompted.

Write together

Open a document in your iCloud library and choose Share document… from its menu. On iPhone and iPad, tap the document’s Share button. Enter the other person’s Apple Account email, choose viewing or editing access, and send them the invitation link.

Older Mac builds may show Create shared copy. Use that action to invite people to a separate copy of the document.

They can open the link in Fei Notes or paste it under Accept invitation in Shared Documents. Use the document’s sharing menu to manage access.

Back up and restore

In Settings → Library, choose Back Up Library or Export library backup and select a destination. The .feinotesbackup file includes documents, attachments, Trash, and changes waiting to sync. It may also include local copies of shared documents.

To restore, select the file under Restore from Backup, then follow the prompts to close shared document windows and restart Fei Notes. Your previous library is kept. Check the restored documents before removing old copies.

Delete documents or stop syncing

Deleted documents stay in Trash for 30 days. To remove one sooner, choose Delete permanently in Trash. After deleting from an iCloud library, choose Sync Now and check the result on your other devices.

To stop syncing, choose Switch to Local… in Settings → Library. This changes the library you are using and keeps the documents already stored in iCloud.

To revoke someone’s access, manage members or stop sharing from the document’s sharing menu. Exported backups and copies saved by other people need to be managed separately too.

Need a hand? Email us or join the Telegram group above. Your Fei Notes version, system version, and a short description of what happened will help us look into it. If you share a screenshot or log, remove any private document content and invitation links first.
