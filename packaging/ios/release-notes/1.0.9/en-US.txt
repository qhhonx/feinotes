Fei Notes 1.0.9 (1021) beta

• Settings provides direct access to the document library, iCloud sync, and Trash. Turn off syncing from the iCloud page.
• The library page shows whether you are using the local or iCloud library. On Mac, choose Show in Finder on the Local data folder row.
• Trash now shows the original location and time remaining. Fixed list clipping on iPad and cases where Empty Trash could fail.
• On iPhone and iPad, rows display the setting and its value side by side, with help text below.

Use sample documents to check updating from 1.0.8, syncing across devices, and the Empty Trash confirmation and result. Cloud features are included during TestFlight testing without a subscription.
