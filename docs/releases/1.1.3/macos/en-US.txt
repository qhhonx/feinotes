Thank you for trying Fei Notes and for every piece of feedback.

If something goes wrong or you have a suggestion, you can reach us here:
• TestFlight: open the screenshot preview and choose Send Beta Feedback from the Share menu. You can also open TestFlight → Fei Notes → Send Beta Feedback and attach a screenshot.
• Telegram feedback group: https://t.me/+eKIYrdIFufxmYzJl
• Website support: https://feinotes.app/en/support/ (support@feinotes.app)
• You can also reply to the social media post or discussion where you found Fei Notes.
Please include your device, OS and app version, and what you did before the issue occurred.

Changes based on your feedback:
• Attachment previews: resize attachments that display preview content in a document, and align them left, centre, or right. Audio previews support width adjustment. Portrait videos continue to show the full frame at its original aspect ratio without cropping.

Other fixes and improvements:
• Reading/editing transitions and the sharing and editing controls in both interface styles.
• Export document and Export board menus. Both Markdown modes and PDF continue to use the macOS save panel; check saving, cancelling, and returning to the document.
• Object-specific sharing labels for documents, boards, and folders, with Copy Link grouped alongside sharing actions.

The separate in-app Print entry has been removed. To print, export a PDF and use a system app.
