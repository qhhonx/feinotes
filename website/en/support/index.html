Start writing

Open Fei Notes and create a note. Local notes work offline and do not require an account.

Import your Quiet.md notes

In Settings → Documents, choose Import from Quiet.md and select your existing library folder. Fei Notes copies the notes and attachments into your current library and keeps the original folder.

Continue on another Mac

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both Macs. In Fei Notes, open Settings → Documents, then the iCloud Sync settings, and follow the prompts. When you first enable sync, you can copy your local notes to iCloud.

On your other Mac, open the same iCloud library and let your notes download. If Fei Notes asks you to restart, quit and reopen the app.

iCloud sync and collaboration are free during TestFlight testing.

A note has not synced yet

Check the sync status in Settings → Documents. Make sure you are connected to the internet, signed in to iCloud, and have enough storage, then choose Sync Now. Changes waiting to upload stay on this Mac.

If you see Items That Couldn’t Sync, follow the message on the original Mac. Check that the attachment is still there and can be read. Replace files larger than 64 MiB with smaller ones. If you changed iCloud accounts, restart Fei Notes when prompted.

Write together

Open a note in your iCloud library and choose Create shared copy from the note menu. In Shared Notes, enter the other person’s Apple Account email, choose Invite to view or Invite to edit, and send them the invitation link.

They can paste the link into Shared Notes in Fei Notes and accept the invitation. You can change permissions or choose Revoke access there. The original note and the shared copy are saved separately.

Back up and restore

In Settings → Documents, find Back Up Library and choose a folder. The .feinotesbackup file includes notes, attachments, Trash, and changes waiting to sync. It may also include local copies of shared notes.

To restore, select the file under Restore from Backup, then follow the prompts to close shared-note windows and restart Fei Notes. Your previous library is kept. Check the restored notes before removing old copies.

Delete notes or stop syncing

Deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days. To remove one sooner, choose Delete permanently in Trash. After deleting from an iCloud library, choose Sync Now and check the result on your other Macs.

To stop syncing, choose Switch to Local… in Settings → Documents. This changes the library you are using and keeps the notes already stored in iCloud.

Revoke access to a shared copy separately in Shared Notes. Exported backups and copies saved by other people need to be managed separately too.

Need a hand? Email us or join the Telegram group above. Your Fei Notes version, macOS version, and a short description of what happened will help us look into it. If you share a screenshot or log, remove any private note content and invitation links first.
