# Mobile sync scene captures

`assets/sync-iphone-en.png` and `assets/sync-iphone-zh.png` are unchanged iPhone
simulator screenshot exports from the integrated Fei Notes application. They use
synthetic seaside notes matching the existing Mac scene and reuse its existing
lighthouse photo. No personal library, account or live collaboration data is
included. The phone frame is CSS around the screenshot.

These images illustrate the application on two device types. They are not a
recording or measurement of a live CloudKit transfer.
